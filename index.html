Tamil Learn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Tamil Learn ("the app"), published by Aramseilearn, is a free educational app for learning the Tamil language — alphabets, words, Thirukkural, Aathichudi, and quizzes. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and no third-party libraries that collect data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your learning progress (quiz stars, unlocked levels, flashcard progress, and settings such as the selected grade) in private on-device storage so the app remembers your progress. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

The app is designed to be safe for children. Because no personal data is collected from anyone, there is nothing to share with third parties.

Network access

The app works fully offline. It does not make any network requests and does not hold the Internet permission.

Pronunciation audio

Letter pronunciations come from audio files bundled inside the app and from your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. No audio is recorded, uploaded, or sent anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects data handling, this policy will be updated and the new version will be linked from the Play Store listing.
