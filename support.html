Frequently asked questions

🔊 How do I hear pronunciations?

Tap the speaker button next to any letter, word, kural, story, or conversation turn. On kids' screens the play buttons sit at the top.

🔇 No sound on iPhone?

First check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone — when it shows orange, the app's audio is muted. Flip it off and turn the volume up. Also make sure Bluetooth headphones aren't connected unexpectedly.

🔠 The text is too small (or too big)

Open Settings → Text Size and choose Normal, Large, or XL. Thirukural couplets always keep their classic 2-line form at any size.

🌙 How do I switch dark mode on/off?

Tap the moon/sun button in the top bar of the home screen. More themes and colors live under Settings.

⭐ How do Favorites work?

Tap the star on any word to favorite it. Starred words appear both in their own category and in the ★ Favorites category at the end of the word list. Tap the star again to remove.

🎙️ I found a wrong pronunciation — can I report it?

Yes, please! Use Contribute Sounds in the app to record the correct pronunciation, or email us at rsadal001@gmail.com with the letter or word and what's wrong.

🔄 How do I reset my child's progress?

Open Settings and use the reset option to clear stars, unlocked levels, and favorites, and start fresh.

💰 Is the app free? Are there accounts or ads?

Completely free. No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases — and the app works fully offline.

👶 Is it safe for children?

Yes. The app collects no personal data from anyone — no accounts, no analytics, no advertising. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Still need help?
